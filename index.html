Supa Multi Chat

One chat for every platform you stream or watch on.

Supa Multi Chat combines live chat from Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok into one window, with 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes, real platform badges and user cards. It comes as a Windows desktop app and a Chrome extension that replaces a stream's own chat.

  • Twitch
  • Kick
  • YouTube
  • TikTok

What it does

  • Reads every platform's chat together — no login needed to read.
  • Finds a streamer's other channels from the links they post.
  • Lets you chat and reply as your own account, one chat at a time — or, for streamers and mods, to all of them at once.
  • Moderators and streamers can time out, ban and unban on the platforms where they're a moderator.
  • Customisable look: fonts, colours, badges, animations and event filters.

Your data

Everything runs on your own computer. There are no Supa Multi Chat servers and no accounts with us: logins are stored on your device and only ever sent to the platform they belong to. Read the privacy policy for the details.
